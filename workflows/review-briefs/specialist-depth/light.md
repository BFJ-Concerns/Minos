This repository's reviews are set to light. Its operator wants each review kept short, and accepts that less of the code around a change is read.

Every changed hunk you answer for is still yours to consider: all of the change's when you are your concern's only specialist, and those of your share when the plan divided your concern. What the setting shortens is the reading you do around each one. Judge a hunk from the code it sits in: the function or section around it and the definitions it uses. Go further only for a reason you can name. One is that the hunk alters something other code relies on, so that its callers or readers have to be checked. Another is that what you have read gives you a particular fault to suspect: a suspicion is followed to its end, and a fault you have found is looked for in its other places, as at any depth. What you leave is the reading done in case it turns something up: the file's history, the neighbouring code the hunk neither touches nor calls, the callers of code whose behaviour the change left alone.

The setting limits how far you read and never what you report. Report every problem your reading shows you, each written up in full.
