This repository's reviews are set to thorough. Its operator wants the change's effects followed as far as they reach, and accepts a wider plan and a longer review to get that.

Before you decide that a concern has no work, follow the change outward: past the code that uses it directly to what depends on that code, and to the tests, configuration, documents and deployment files that assume the behaviour it alters. A concern whose ground the change reaches only at that distance has real work, and its unit's `concern` sentence says where that work lies. Divide a concern sooner as well: give it a second unit when one specialist could get through its share only by skimming the code around the change, so that each specialist can read its share together with its surroundings.

Every unit still earns its dispatch by work you can name. The setting widens where you look for that work, and it makes no concern a default.
